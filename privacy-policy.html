Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CareWell Pharmacy ("CareWell", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information used by our pharmacy management software.

1. Information We Handle

CareWell may process information that a pharmacy chooses to enter into the application, including pharmacy records, medicine information, inventory information, customer records, supplier information, sales records, purchase records, expense records and related business information.

Users are responsible for ensuring that information entered into CareWell is collected and used lawfully.

2. Google Account and Google Drive

CareWell may provide an optional Google Drive backup feature. When a user chooses to connect Google Drive, Google OAuth is used to authorize the connection.

CareWell does not ask users for or store their Google account password.

The Google Drive integration is intended to create and manage CareWell backup files associated with the user's use of the application.

3. Google Drive Permission

CareWell uses the Google Drive permission required to create and manage files used by the application. The application does not need unrestricted access to all files in a user's Google Drive for the CareWell backup feature.

4. Backup Data

When a user chooses to create a backup, CareWell may store pharmacy application data in a CareWell backup file in the user's connected Google Drive account.

Users should review their Google Drive account and backup history regularly and maintain appropriate access security for their Google account.

5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

CareWell does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information for advertising purposes. We do not sell pharmacy business data or customer information to advertisers.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by CareWell. However, no software, computer system or online storage service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

CareWell may use third-party services such as Google APIs for features that the user explicitly enables. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

8. User Control

Users can choose whether to connect Google Drive. Users can disconnect the Google Drive connection from the CareWell application and can manage their Google Account permissions through Google's account settings.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

CareWell is designed to allow pharmacy users to manage their application data and backup files. Google Drive backup files remain under the user's Google Drive account unless the user deletes them or they are otherwise removed according to the user's account settings.

10. Children's Privacy

CareWell is business software intended for pharmacies and professional users. It is not directed toward children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when CareWell's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
